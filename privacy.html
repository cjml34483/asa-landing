1. Who we are

ASA Terminal is a Windows desktop application developed and operated by ASA Trading Systems Ltd, trading as ASA Systems, a company incorporated in England and Wales, registered in Bristol, United Kingdom.

ASA Trading Systems Ltd is the data controller for personal data processed in connection with the ASA Terminal application and website (asasystems.app).

Contact: christoffermoshiri@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

2.1 Account data

When you create an account we collect:

  • Email address and display name
  • Password (stored as a bcrypt hash — we never store your plaintext password)
  • OAuth identifiers if you sign in via Google, Discord, or Telegram (we receive a unique ID and your public profile email from those providers)
  • Billing address and payment method metadata provided by Stripe (we do not store card numbers; Stripe holds those)
  • Subscription tier and status
  • Timestamps: account created, last login, password last changed

2.2 Product telemetry (opt-in)

ASA Terminal includes optional, anonymised product analytics via PostHog. This is disabled by default and only activated if you opt in. When enabled, we collect feature usage counts and error rates — no trading data, no signal content, no credentials, no personally identifiable information.

2.3 Support communications

If you contact us by email, we retain the content of that correspondence to resolve your query and improve the product.

2.4 Data we do NOT collect from the application

Your trading data stays on your machine. Trade records, signal history, journal notes, broker credentials, Telegram/Discord session tokens, and MetaTrader 5 account details are stored exclusively in a local SQLite database on your device. None of this data is transmitted to ASA Systems servers.

4. Legal bases (UK & EU GDPR)

We rely on the following legal bases under UK GDPR (and, where applicable, EU GDPR) Article 6:

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): Processing necessary to provide the ASA Terminal service you signed up for — account management, authentication, subscription handling.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Security monitoring, fraud detection, improving service reliability, and responding to support queries. We have balanced these interests against your rights and concluded they are proportionate.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): Compliance with applicable UK law, tax obligations, and any court or regulatory orders.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): Optional product analytics. You can withdraw consent at any time from within the application settings.

5. Data stored on your device

The vast majority of data generated by ASA Terminal is stored locally on your Windows device in a SQLite database. This includes:

  • Trade records, signal history, and provider attribution data
  • Journal entries, notes, and screenshots
  • Broker connection configuration (Fernet-encrypted at rest)
  • Telegram and Discord session credentials (encrypted)
  • Risk management settings and backtesting results

You are responsible for the security of your own device. ASA Systems does not have remote access to this data and cannot recover it if your device is lost or damaged. We recommend maintaining your own backups.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

Access (Art. 15)

Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

Rectification (Art. 16)

Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

Erasure (Art. 17)

Request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

Portability (Art. 20)

Receive your data in a machine-readable format (JSON/CSV).

Restriction (Art. 18)

Ask us to restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.

Objection (Art. 21)

Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Withdraw consent

Opt out of analytics at any time in the app settings.

Complaint

Lodge a complaint with the ICO (ico.org.uk).

You can exercise your rights for access, portability, and erasure directly within ASA Terminal (Settings → Account → Privacy). For other requests or questions, contact us at christoffermoshiri@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Cookies & analytics

10.1 Website (asasystems.app)

Our marketing website uses only strictly necessary cookies required for security and session management. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels on the marketing site.

10.2 Application analytics (PostHog)

The desktop application can optionally send anonymised product telemetry to PostHog. This feature is off by default. If you enable it, PostHog receives events such as "feature X opened" or "error Y occurred" — no trading data, no signal content, and no personal identifiers are included in these events.

You can enable or disable this at any time in the application settings.

11. Children

ASA Terminal is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will delete the account promptly.
