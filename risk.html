2. Nature of the Risks

Market Risk

Prices of financial instruments can move rapidly and unpredictably. Adverse price movements can result in losses that equal or exceed your entire invested capital. Past price performance is not a reliable indicator of future results.

Leverage Risk

CFDs and forex products are leveraged instruments. Leverage amplifies both gains and losses relative to your margin deposit. A small adverse movement in the underlying market can result in a loss that significantly exceeds the amount you initially deposited.

Signal and Automation Risk

ASA Terminal automates the copying of third-party trading signals. Signal providers have their own track records, methodologies, and risk profiles. Past performance of any signal provider does not guarantee future results. You remain solely responsible for selecting which signals to follow and configuring appropriate risk parameters.

Technology and Connectivity Risk

Automated trading systems depend on reliable internet connectivity, the continued availability of MetaTrader 5, and the correct functioning of ASA Terminal software. Technical failures, connectivity interruptions, or software errors may result in missed trades, duplicate trades, or unintended positions.

Liquidity Risk

Some markets, particularly exotic currency pairs and certain CFD instruments, can become illiquid. During periods of low liquidity, spreads may widen significantly and it may not be possible to execute trades at expected prices.

Counterparty and Broker Risk

Your trades are executed through your chosen broker. You are exposed to the financial health and regulatory standing of that broker. ASA Systems has no control over, and accepts no responsibility for, the conduct of your broker.

4. Past Performance

Any performance data shown in ASA Terminal — including per-provider Sharpe ratios, win rates, R-multiples, or equity curves — reflects historical results only. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future results. Markets are dynamic and historical patterns can change or reverse without warning.

5. Seek Independent Advice

Before trading leveraged products, you should carefully consider your investment objectives, level of experience, and risk appetite. You should seek independent financial advice from an FCA-authorised adviser if you are unsure whether trading is right for you.

If you are in the United Kingdom and wish to find a regulated financial adviser, you can use the FCA's Financial Services Register at register.fca.org.uk.
