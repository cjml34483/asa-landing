Security & Trust

How We Protect Your Data

ASA Systems is built with security as a first-class concern. Here is how we handle your data and protect your account.

🔒

Encryption in Transit

All data between your browser, the ASA Terminal app, and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher. We do not support unencrypted connections.

🗄️

Encryption at Rest

Data stored in our database is encrypted at rest using AES-256. Database backups are also encrypted.

🏗️

SOC 2 Infrastructure

Our backend runs on Supabase, which operates on SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure. Physical and logical access controls are audited independently.

🛡️

No MT5 Credentials on Servers

Your MetaTrader 5 login, password, and server details never leave your machine. ASA Terminal connects to MT5 locally via the MetaTrader 5 Python bridge only.

📊

Minimal Data Collection

We collect only what is necessary to operate the service. Telemetry is anonymised, aggregated, and opt-in — disabled by default. No trading data or credentials leave your device.

🔑

Access Controls

Production database access is restricted to authorised personnel only, using role-based permissions enforced at the database level via Supabase Row Level Security (RLS).

What We Store

When you create an account with ASA Systems, we store:

  • Email address — used for account authentication and important service notifications
  • Subscription status — your current plan tier, managed via Stripe
  • Anonymised usage telemetry — feature usage counts and error rates, only if you have opted in

We do not store your MetaTrader 5 credentials, your trading history, your positions, your signal channel content, or any personally identifiable trading data.

What We Do Not Store

  • MT5 account login, password, or server details
  • Telegram or Discord account tokens or session data
  • Signal channel messages or content
  • Trade history, positions, or P&L data
  • Any financial account information

All of the above remains entirely on your local machine.

Responsible Disclosure

We take security vulnerabilities seriously. If you believe you have found a security issue in ASA Terminal or our website, please report it to us responsibly before disclosing it publicly.

Report security vulnerabilities to: christoffermoshiri@gmail.com
Please include a description of the issue, steps to reproduce, and your contact details. We aim to respond within 48 hours and will work with you to address confirmed issues promptly.

We ask that you give us reasonable time to investigate and remediate before any public disclosure. We will not pursue legal action against researchers who act in good faith under these guidelines.
