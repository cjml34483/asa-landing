Security & Trust

How We Protect Your Data

ASA Systems is built with security as a first-class concern. Here is how we handle your data and protect your account.

🔒

Encrypted in Transit

All communication between your devices and our services is encrypted end-to-end using modern, industry-standard protocols.

🗄️

Encrypted at Rest

Any data we hold on your behalf is encrypted at rest using industry-standard encryption, including all backups.

🏗️

Enterprise-Grade Infrastructure

We rely on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure operated by providers with independent security certifications and regular third-party audits.

🛡️

Credentials Stay on Your Device

Your trading account credentials never leave your machine. ASA Terminal connects to your broker locally — we never see, transmit, or store your login details.

📊

Minimal Data Collection

We collect only what is necessary to operate the service. Any optional telemetry is anonymised, aggregated, and opt-in — disabled by default.

🔑

Strict Access Controls

Access to your data is strictly controlled, logged, and audited. Only a small number of authorised personnel can reach production systems, and only when strictly necessary.

What Stays on Your Device

ASA Terminal is designed to keep sensitive information local. Your trading credentials, positions, trade history, signal channel content, and any P&L data remain entirely on your own machine. None of it is transmitted to or stored by ASA Systems.

What We Hold

To run your account we hold only the minimum required — for example, the email address you signed up with and your current subscription status. We do not collect or retain personally identifiable trading data.

Responsible Disclosure

We take security seriously and welcome reports from researchers acting in good faith. If you believe you have found a security issue, please get in touch with us privately before any public disclosure.
