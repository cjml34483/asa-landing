Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 May 2026

This policy explains how refunds work for ASA Terminal subscriptions purchased from asasystems.app. It sits alongside our Terms of Service and applies to all customers worldwide; UK and EU consumers also have the statutory rights described in section 1.

1. UK / EU 14-day cooling-off right

Under the UK Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013, consumers in the UK have the right to cancel a distance contract for a service within 14 days of entering into it, without giving any reason.

Important. ASA Terminal is supplied as a digital service. Under regulation 37, the 14-day cooling-off right is lost once you have given express consent for the service to begin and acknowledged that doing so means losing the right. When you start your free trial and connect ASA Terminal to MetaTrader 5, Telegram, or Discord, the service has begun and the cooling-off right ends.

If you have not yet started the service (for example, you subscribed but never installed or opened the app), you may cancel within 14 days of subscribing and receive a full refund of any amount already charged. To exercise this right, email support@asasystems.app from the email address on your account within 14 days of purchase, with the subject line Cooling-off cancellation.

2. Refunds outside the 14-day window

Once your trial has begun or you are inside an active paid period, subscription fees are non-refundable for periods that have already been used. We will consider partial-period refund requests in the following situations, on a case-by-case basis:

  • A duplicate or accidental charge.
  • A documented service outage on our end that prevented you from using ASA Terminal for a continuous period of 48 hours or more.
  • A billing error that resulted in an incorrect amount being taken.

To request a refund in any of these cases, email support@asasystems.app with your account email and a short description of what happened. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

3. How long refunds take

Where a refund is approved, we will process it back to the original payment method via Stripe within 14 days of approval. Depending on your bank, the funds may take up to a further 5 working days to appear in your account. We do not charge any administrative fee for processing a refund.

4. Cancelling future renewals (no refund needed)

You can cancel auto-renewal at any time and keep your access until the end of your current paid period — no refund is required because no future charge is taken. To cancel:

  • From the ASA Terminal app: Settings → Account → Manage Subscription.
  • From the website: My Account → Manage Subscription (opens the Stripe Customer Portal).
  • Or email support@asasystems.app and we will cancel on your behalf.

5. Chargebacks

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us first at support@asasystems.app. We can usually resolve disputes faster than your bank. Filing a chargeback without contacting us may result in the associated subscription being immediately cancelled and the account being closed pending resolution.

6. What is non-refundable

  • Any billing period that has been actively used (your trades have been copied, the dashboard has loaded, the app has connected to a broker or signal source).
  • Add-on services purchased through third parties (for example, an AI provider API key billed by Anthropic, Google, or another vendor).
  • Losses or missed gains resulting from trading activity. ASA Systems provides analytical software only and is not authorised by the FCA; please review our Risk Disclosure before using the app for real-money trading.

7. Contact

All refund and billing questions: support@asasystems.app
